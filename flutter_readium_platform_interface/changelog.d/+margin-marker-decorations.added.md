**Margin marker decorations.**
`DecorationStyle.marginMarker` shows a text label or a built-in icon (bookmark, note or star) in the page margin, level with the first line of a locator, on iOS, Android and web.
Use it for printed page numbers or bookmarked highlights. See the [margin markers guide](https://github.com/Notalib/flutter_readium/blob/main/docs/guides/margin-markers.md).
