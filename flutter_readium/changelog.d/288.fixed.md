**Android and iOS: opening a streamed Web Publication no longer probes every chapter before the reader appears.**
The reader asked every chapter for its content length up front — at least one HEAD request per chapter before first paint, which could stall opening large books over the network. Positions for streamed publications are now one per chapter instead of byte-precise pages, so `Locator.locations.position` steps per chapter on both platforms; `Locator.totalProgression` stays continuous on iOS (interpolated within the chapter) but becomes chapter-granular on Android. Local (zip) publications are unchanged.
